How to Test SaveBot

A quick, no-jargon walkthrough so you can try the app end to end and tell me what feels great and what feels off. This is an early test build — the core experience is here; a couple of things are still coming (noted at the end).

SaveBot is your personal place to save posts from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and more — and it uses AI to turn each one into a clean, organized card (a recipe, a workout, a travel list, and so on) that you can actually find and use later.

How to open it on your iPhone

For now, test SaveBot in Safari on your iPhone — just open the web link I sent you. Tip: tap the Share icon in Safari → Add to Home Screen so it opens full-screen like a real app. The installable iPhone app is coming next via TestFlight; this web version lets you try almost everything today.

Give AI saves a few seconds — you'll see a short "Processing…" state, then the finished card.

1Get in

  1. Open the web link in Safari.
  2. Create an account or sign in. You can use email, Sign in with Google, or Sign in with Apple — try whichever you'd normally use.
  3. Confirm you land on your library after signing in, and that signing out and back in remembers you.

2First-time setup (onboarding)

  1. On a brand-new account you'll get a few quick questions — what kind of content you save (recipes, workouts, travel…), which apps you save from, and how you like things organized.
  2. Answer honestly — the app uses this to arrange your categories for you.
  3. Check that after setup, your categories match what you picked (the stuff you care about shows first).

3Save something (the main event)

On the web version, use Add to save things. (Sharing straight from Instagram/TikTok needs the installable app — see the note below.)

  1. Tap the Add button.
  2. Paste a link — copy the share link of a public TikTok or YouTube video and paste it in. Watch it process, then open the finished card.
  3. Paste or type text — e.g. paste a recipe or some notes and let the AI organize it.
  4. Upload a photo — e.g. a screenshot of a recipe; the AI reads the text and builds a card.
  5. For each one, open the result and check the AI got it roughly right — title, summary, key details, a sensible category, and tags.

Two things to expect

1. Sharing from other apps (tap Share in TikTok → SaveBot) works in the installable app, not the web version — it's coming to your iPhone via TestFlight. For now, "paste a link" tests the exact same save → AI → card flow.

2. TikTok and YouTube pull in the best right now. Instagram and Facebook are limited in this build (they lock their posts down), so don't be surprised if those come back thin — that's expected, not a bug.

4Look at the card two ways

  1. Inside an item, switch between Clean View (the tidy AI version) and Original View (the post as it was, with the video playing inline where possible).
  2. Confirm the toggle works and the app remembers which view you prefer.

5Find and organize

  1. Use Search at the top — type a word from something you saved and check it comes up. Try the filter chips (by category, type, platform, date).
  2. Browse your categories, switch between grid and list, and check the Recent and Favorites tabs.
  3. Try managing categories — rename, reorder, hide, or add a new one — and make sure your items still show where you'd expect.

6Recipe Cook Mode

  1. Open any recipe you saved and start Cook Mode.
  2. Check the big, kitchen-friendly text, that the screen stays awake, that you can move through the steps, use the per-step timer, and adjust servings.

7Edit & tidy an item

  1. On any saved item, try favorite, archive, and delete.
  2. Edit its notes, tweak tags, and change its category — and confirm the changes stick.

8What's not ready yet

So you know what to skip for now — these are coming, not broken:

  • The installable iPhone app (with share-from-other-apps saving) — coming next via TestFlight. For now it's the web version in Safari.
  • Payments / subscriptions — the last piece we'll build. Everything is free to use in this test.
  • Instagram / Facebook full content — limited on purpose for now.
  • Full photo carousels & video transcripts — we save the main thumbnail today; grabbing every image and the spoken words needs a bit more plumbing.
  • Sharing to friends, profiles, following — later roadmap.

How to tell me what you find

For anything that looks off, jot down three things: what you did, what you expected, and what actually happened — a screenshot or screen recording is gold. Small stuff counts too (wording, spacing, anything confusing).

Thanks for testing 🙏 — take your time, poke at everything, and be brutally honest. This is exactly how we make it great before launch.
